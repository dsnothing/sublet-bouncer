# Limitations

- **Synthetic model/data:** the logistic scam model trains on seeded synthetic examples. Its score is uncalibrated and cannot establish a real-world fraud probability. Gold fixtures overlap the authors' rule knowledge; blind v2 is reported separately. See [sources](DATA-SOURCES.md) and [evaluation](../evals/RESULTS.md).
- **Transit proxy:** the November 24, 2026 GRT weekday schedule is a current-term proxy for Winter 2027. Fixed departure and cached arrival times do not optimize a personal itinerary or reflect disruptions. Straight-line walks omit barriers and accessibility; same-stop transfers have no minimum buffer. See `data/transit_provenance.json` and the transit builder described in [sources](DATA-SOURCES.md).
- **Rent proxy:** CMHC/Statistics Canada values are means, despite the compatibility key `median_monthly`. Room rent is half a two-bedroom mean, not a measured room median or today's asking rent.
- **Geocoding:** 16 cached entries cover only selected Waterloo-area locations. Some are streets/places, not verified civic addresses. Unknown addresses escalate; matching aliases and nearest origins remain approximate ([core/engine.ts:40](../core/engine.ts#L40)).
- **English and Ontario only:** input refusal uses language heuristics, not reliable language identification. Plain/simple English display does not add multilingual analysis. Ontario rules cover selected issues; other jurisdictions and legal exceptions are outside scope ([core/injection.ts](../core/injection.ts)).
- **Quota and latency:** Gemini 429/503 responses and timeouts can cause a fallback model or offline rules. The adapter budgets 14 seconds for primary and 6 for fallback within 20 total ([api/gemini.ts](../api/gemini.ts)). [Production evidence](../evidence/c2c-production.json) records two successful Lite responses at one deployed SHA; it does not establish sustained reliability or a latency guarantee.
- **Dated legislation:** [RTA verification](../data/RTA-VERIFICATION.md) records retrieval on November 24, 2026. Archived excerpts do not verify future amendments. Sharing kitchen/bath is only one applicability exception; patterns can miss negation, voluntary payments and context. Not legal advice.
- **Trust and recovery:** no ownership, identity or listing authenticity verification. ADMIT means no implemented check blocked the listing. Undo restores local state, not external clipboard/download effects; logs and localStorage are editable by the browser user.
