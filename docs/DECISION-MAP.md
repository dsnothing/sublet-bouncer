# Decision map

Implementation: [engine](../core/engine.ts), [verification](../core/extract.ts), [action and recovery policy](../core/log.ts). Confidence values are policy annotations, not calibrated correctness probabilities. D5 is computed before D2 but emitted after D4.

| ID | Inputs ? output | Method and reason | Threshold and escalation | Failure mode | Covering test names |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Listing ? fields with original spans | LLM handles varied wording; rules supply offline extraction; verifier rejects unsupported evidence | Field confidence below 0.8, missing rent, invalid evidence or injection ? review | Real span can still be misunderstood; schema failure falls back | `drops a fabricated span and escalates`; `escalates low-confidence live fields`; `flags injection and never admits it` (core.test.ts) |
| D2 | Listing, proposals, applicability, rule table ? clause findings | Deterministic rules anchor known sections; LLM only proposes known IDs for paraphrases | Exact 1, fuzzy 0.6, proposal 0.7; below 0.75 ? review; void downgraded when applicability unconfirmed | Negation/exception errors and missed paraphrases | `finds damage deposit under verified s105` (core.test.ts); `escalates a verified unmatched proposal at 0.7 and never marks it void` (classification.test.ts) |
| D3 | Listing features, fields, rent proxy, fixed weights ? score/band/top contributions | Logistic model makes weighted signals inspectable | Below 0.3 proceed; 0.3?0.7 inclusive ? review; above 0.7 block | Synthetic-only training, uncalibrated score | `is deterministic and shares training/serve feature order`; `payment before viewing forces REFUSE even with low model risk` (core.test.ts) |
| D4 | Address, campus, class time, cached geocodes and routes ? commute | Cached rules avoid invented routes | Geocode at least 0.8, origin at most 600m; arrival margin ?10 feasible, ?0 tight, otherwise infeasible; unknown ? review | Stale schedules, approximate walks; unknown address | `uses tight and infeasible arrival boundaries`; `unknown address escalates and blocks ICS` (core.test.ts) |
| D5 | owner_lives_in field ? applies / may_not_apply / unknown | Explicit sharing rule exposes one key exception | Display threshold 0.8; branching uses field value, not that threshold; unknown ? kitchen/bath question | Owner residence is an imperfect sharing proxy; other exceptions absent | `shared kitchen with owner makes clauses questionable`; `unknown RTA applicability cannot silently become applies` (core.test.ts) |
| D6 | Refusal, payment signal, score, escalations, clauses, commute ? verdict/reasons | Explicit rules keep precedence inspectable | Refused input/payment before viewing/score >0.7 ? REFUSE; open questions/non-ok clauses/infeasible commute ? HOLD; otherwise ADMIT | ADMIT cannot establish authenticity | `admits a clean listing with seven typed decisions`; `payment before viewing forces REFUSE even with low model risk` (core.test.ts) |
| D7 | Result and profile ? action status/tier | Rules enforce narrow capabilities | ICS: feasible/tight, score <0.3, no escalations, input not refused; confirmation required | Local permission state is not account authorization | `requires human perform to complete ICS`; `blocks a stale proposed ICS once risk changes` (core.test.ts) |

Test files: [core](../tests/core.test.ts), [classification](../tests/classification.test.ts), [API](../tests/api.test.ts), [latency](../tests/latency.test.ts). Missing provider/configuration, malformed extraction and timeouts produce a visible offline-fallback reason. No LLM chooses the final verdict or grants permission.
